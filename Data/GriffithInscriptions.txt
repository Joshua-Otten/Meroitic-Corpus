# MEROITIC_INSCRIPTIONS_GRIFFITH
# Inscriptions printed in Meroitic type in F. Ll. Griffith, "Meroitic Inscriptions" I (1911) and II (1912), and in Garstang, Sayce and Griffith, "Meroe, the City of the Ethiopians" (1911). The readings are Griffith's, in the public domain; scans at archive.org (archaeologicals19egyp, archaeologicals20egyp, merocityofethiop00gars).
# Transcribed from the printed type by a language model (Claude Opus 5.5) on 2026-11-09, not by hand. In a test on 12 of these texts, read before the modern edition was opened, 95.6% of signs agreed with the modern readings in Fontes Historiae Nubiorum (FHN) and 99.6% with what Griffith printed; the test's two misreadings, and a third found later in no. 111 (qoleb, read qleb), are corrected here. On those texts Griffith's readings differ from the modern (REM) ones in about 4% of signs. The test covered only cursive type from Meroitic Inscriptions II (labels and graffiti at Philae); 41 of the texts here are in hieroglyphic type.
# (Spelled as in mero-corpus.txt, MilletExamples.txt and Kalabsha_Millet.txt: E-hat transcribed 'E'; 4th H transcribed as 'X'; 3rd H transcribed as 'x'; S-hat transcribed as 'S'; the se sign as 's'; N-tilda transcribed as 'N'. Griffith's z is 'd'.)
# Left out: word dividers, line numbers, the marks of uncertain signs, and signs with no letter value (Egyptian signs, numerals and marks of unknown value); numerals with a value are digits. A '.' stands for a lost sign or one of Griffith's dots for a gap, so dots do not always count the lost signs; gaps between words are left out. Signs Griffith restored in square brackets are dots in Meroitic Inscriptions I and nos. 75-105 of II, and letters in nos. 106-137 of II and in Meroe.
# Each inscription is one line, under a comment line with the edition, Griffith's number, the REM number where known, the site and the page (nN is the page of the archive.org scan, archive.org/details/<id>/page/nN). Here are 123 of the 152 transcribed; the other 29, which Griffith printed only in part (for example words quoted in his commentary, or a row of a table), are left out. Of three texts here too he printed only some lines: Meroitic Inscriptions I no. 21 (line 1 of 11), II no. 80 (not the words in the field) and II no. 81 (lines 1-3 of 5).
